PaaS Exit Playbook

Render free instances spin down: what replaces them on a VPS

Render's free web services are convenient for demos, with one well known catch: they spin down after a period without traffic and take a while to wake up on the next request. For a portfolio project that is fine. For anything users rely on, the cold start becomes the main complaint.

Why it happens

Free instances are shared capacity. Stopping idle services is how the free tier stays free. Paid instances stay running.

The workaround people try

Pinging the service every few minutes keeps it awake, but it works against the intent of the free tier and free usage limits still apply. It is better to choose a setup that matches the workload.

What a small VPS gives you

A low cost VPS can run several hobby apps at once, always on, with no cold starts:

  • Each app as a container behind one reverse proxy with automatic TLS.
  • A shared Postgres instance with a database per app.
  • Restart policies so apps come back after a reboot.

Memory is the limit. Five small Node or Python apps with a shared database fit comfortably in 2 GB.

What you take on

Updates, backups and monitoring become your job. A self-hosted PaaS or a few scripts make that manageable, but it is not zero work.

A reasonable split

Keep throwaway demos on free tiers. Move anything with real users, or anything that needs background jobs, to an always-on server.
